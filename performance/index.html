Understanding the Metrics:
  • Accuracy: Percentage of matches where the predicted winner won.
  • Log Loss: Penalizes confident incorrect predictions (lower is better, optimal ~0.500-0.600).
  • Brier Score: Mean squared difference between probability and actual result (0 = perfect, lower is better).
  • Calibration Error: Measures if a 70% prediction wins 70% of the time in practice.
